# Community code of conduct

We want Ticketloop to be useful to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Be respectful, specific, and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people. Do not harass, threaten, insult, discriminate, publish private information, or use sexualized language or attention.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that break these rules. Repeated or serious abuse may lead to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct problems privately through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/the-good-pixel/ticketloop/security/advisories/new) and start the report title with `Conduct report`. Do not open a public issue. Maintainers will limit report details to the people needed to handle the case.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is representing the Ticketloop project.
